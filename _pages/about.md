I obtained my BSc from the College of Energy Engineering at Zhejiang University, mentored by Prof. [Kuochih Chuang](https://scholars.lib.ntu.edu.tw/entities/person/d6ba082b-354e-4c9f-bf32-cafab97e5c7a), who transformed my perspective on academia during a time when I hated school.

My current research focuses on advanced manipulation dexterity with dexterous hands and humanoids. I'm particular interested in sim-to-real RL, learning from human video, and generative world models.  Previously, my colleague and I achieved [sensorimotor precise aggressive maneuvers](https://tianyueh8erobot.github.io/research/) (to appear on Science Robotics as a Research Article) in aerial robots beyond human pilot's capabilities.  I hope to see manual-dexterity-level autonomy in machines (as it is unique to human now), through which we can explore the laws, principles, and limitations of artificial and natural intelligence.  

I enjoy making friends, and following soccer and table tennis games. I'm also interested in hip-hop culture.
